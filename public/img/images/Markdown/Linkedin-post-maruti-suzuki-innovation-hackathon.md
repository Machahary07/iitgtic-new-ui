We're thrilled to share the successful conclusion of the Maruti Suzuki Innovation Hackathon, held on March 10, 2026, in collaboration with the Technology Incubation Centre, IIT Guwahati (IITG-TIC).

60+ young innovators across 12 teams pitched bold ideas to a jury comprising top officials from Maruti Suzuki India Limited and IIM Calcutta Innovation Park. And the energy in the room was electric!

After an exciting round of presentations and evaluations, three teams emerged as winners, along with a special jury recognition.

-        Winner: Avinash & Rithvik
-        Runner-Up: Devraj Choudhary & Saksham Barnwal
-        2nd Runner-Up: Himanta Doley
-        Jury Award – Most Unique Idea: Sachin Kumar Sharma

A heartfelt thank you to Maruti Suzuki India Limited, IITG-TIC, IIT Guwahati's Students’ Academic Board and every participant who made this celebration of innovation truly remarkable.
