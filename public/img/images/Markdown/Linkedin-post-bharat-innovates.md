From the labs of Indian Institute of Technology, Guwahati to the global innovation stage.

Proud to see Quant Solar, an IITG Technology Incubation Centre (IITG-TIC) incubated startup, representing India’s innovation ecosystem at Bharat Innovates in Nice, France - a global showcase featuring 120 startups across 13 frontier sectors, selected from institutions across the country.

QuantSolar’s presence reflects the growing strength of IIT Guwahati’s research and innovation ecosystem and the readiness of our startups to compete on the global stage. At IITG Technology Incubation Centre (IITG-TIC), we are committed to transforming research into market-ready technologies that address global challenges in clean energy, climate resilience and sustainability.

Beyond the showcase, Bharat Innovates provides a platform to engage with European universities, research institutions, industry leaders, investors and policymakers, opening new avenues for technology validation, research collaboration, commercialisation and international market access.

Congratulations to the QuantSolar team on this significant milestone. We look forward to many more IITG-TIC startups taking Indian innovation to the world.
