We are pleased to share the successful organization of Battle Blitz 2026, a dynamic Startup Pitching Competition held on 27th April 2026 at the Technology Incubation Centre, IIT Guwahati (IITG-TIC). The event was organized through a collaborative effort by IITG Technology Incubation Centre (IITG-TIC), Smile Foundation, Assam Startup, and Apun NGO.

The competition brought together a diverse and talented pool of participants, with 20+ innovative ideas pitched by bright young minds, startups, and aspiring entrepreneurs from various universities and colleges across Assam. The event served as a vibrant platform to showcase creativity, entrepreneurial vision, and problem-solving capabilities, reflecting the growing innovation ecosystem in the region.

A distinguished jury panel evaluated the pitches, recognizing creativity, potential, and entrepreneurial spirit:

• Trailukya Dutta, Founder of Tholua Pratishthan, a visionary leader dedicated to social impact and grassroots innovation. His work continues to inspire emerging changemakers across the region.

• Kaushik Khanikar , Consultant at Assam Startup, who plays a pivotal role in nurturing and supporting emerging startups. His deep understanding of the startup ecosystem brought valuable insights to the evaluation process.

• Urmi Buragohain, CEO of the Technology Incubation Centre, IIT Guwahati, whose leadership and commitment to fostering innovation have been instrumental in strengthening entrepreneurial initiatives.

The event witnessed enthusiastic participation, engaging discussions, and high-quality pitches, highlighting the immense potential of Assam’s startup landscape. Battle Blitz 2026 stands as a testament to the growing momentum of innovation and entrepreneurship in the region.

We extend our sincere gratitude to all participants, partners, and jury members for contributing to the success of this initiative.
