🚀 mc² + IGNITE | Strengthening the Startup Ecosystem ( held on 10th sept 2026) 

The successful conclusion of mc² + IGNITE – India’s Flagship Energy Innovation Accelerator at IIT Guwahati brought together startups, students, entrepreneurs, policymakers, PSUs, industry leaders, investors and academia on one platform.

The IITG Technology Incubation Centre (IITG-TIC) and Indian Institute of Technology, Guwahati were proud to be partners in this impactful initiative, creating meaningful connections between innovation, academia, industry and entrepreneurship.

🌱 A Vision for the Startup Ecosystem
Hon’ble Chief Minister of Assam, Dr Himanta Biswa Sarma, highlighted the immense potential of entrepreneurship and technology-led innovation and emphasized the need for a stronger and collaborative ecosystem to drive growth and create opportunities.

His vision closely aligns with IITG-TIC’s mission of helping innovative ideas transform into sustainable and scalable ventures.

⛽ PSUs & Startup Collaboration
The participation of Oil India Limited and other energy-sector PSUs highlighted the important role of industry in supporting startups through real-world challenges, technology validation, pilot opportunities and deployment.

🎓 IITG-TIC: Connecting Innovation with Opportunity
At IITG Technology Incubation Centre (IITG-TIC), we work to bridge the gap between research, technology, entrepreneurship and industry by nurturing startups and creating opportunities for mentorship, partnerships, funding and scale.

🔥 Startup Expo
The Startup Expo showcased innovative solutions from emerging ventures. Hon’ble Chief Minister Dr. Himanta Biswa Sarma visited the Expo, interacted with founders and explored their innovations.

💬 Founder Perspectives
Founders from Quant Solar, MadVR Solutions Private Limited and Ecoet Technologies participated in a panel discussion covering startup opportunities, challenges, investment, the role of incubators, industry collaboration and pathways to scale.

💡 Learning from the Experts
Ashok Jhunjhunwala, IIT Madras, spoke about how startups and innovation can address some of India’s large and complex challenges.

Senthilmurugan Subbiah shared practical insights on starting a startup, founder commitment, product development, innovation and the role of incubators.

📊 With 1,500+ Participants, 100+ startups and around 1,000 students, the response demonstrated the growing entrepreneurial energy and enthusiasm.

For IITG-TIC, mc² + IGNITE reinforced our commitment to:
Nurture ideas. Support founders. Connect academia with industry. Enable innovation. Help startups scale.
