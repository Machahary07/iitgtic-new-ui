🚀 Transforming Ideas into AI-Powered Solutions: AI-Assisted Coding Training Program at IITG-TIC (1-5 June, 2026)

The AI revolution is reshaping how we innovate, build, and solve problems. To empower professionals with skills for this new era, IITG Technology Incubation Centre (IITG-TIC) successfully conducted an intensive AI-Assisted Coding Training Program for participants from Numaligarh Refinery Limited.

🔹 Day 1: Building the Foundation
The program commenced with an inaugural session, followed by an insightful talk by Urmi Buragohain on the Innovation and Incubation Ecosystem.
Participants explored:
✅ Fundamentals of AI-assisted development and Vibe Coding
 ✅ Introduction to Large Language Models (LLMs)
 ✅ The evolving AI ecosystem and its impact on innovation
 ✅ Networking and peer learning opportunities

🔹 Day 2: From Learning to Building
Participants transitioned from concepts to creation using Cursor AI and Claude Code for rapid prototyping and project development.
Highlights included:
✅ Software and Product Development Lifecycle
 ✅ Hands-on AI-assisted project development
 ✅ Building AI-enabled products and solutions
 ✅ Interactive discussions with Prof. Senthil Kumar Murugan and Neeraj Sharma on AI, innovation, and entrepreneurship

🔹 Day 3: Data, Machine Learning & Innovation
Participants gained deeper insights into AI foundations through sessions by Senthilmurugan Subbiah covering:
✅ Data fundamentals and machine learning concepts
 ✅ Machine learning algorithms and applications
 ✅ Data preprocessing techniques

The day also featured an inspiring session by Dr. Geetha Manjunath, Founder, CEO & CTO of Niramai Health Analytix, who shared her entrepreneurial journey, lessons from building a deep-tech healthcare startup, and the role of innovation in creating impact.

The enthusiasm and commitment displayed by participants have been remarkable. It is inspiring to see professionals embracing AI as a catalyst for innovation, productivity, and problem-solving.

🚀 AI-Assisted Coding Training Program at IITG-TIC – Day 4 Highlights
Day 4 of the AI-Assisted Coding Training Program at IITG-TIC focused on the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Industry Applications, providing participants with valuable insights into both domain-specific and foundational AI concepts.

🔹 Expert Session by Senthilmurugan Subbiah

Prof. Senthilmurugan Subbiah delivered an engaging session on the transformative role of Artificial Intelligence in Chemical Engineering and Process Industries. Participants explored:
✅ Applications of AI in chemical engineering technologies
 ✅ Industry use cases and AI-driven process optimization
 ✅ Mathematical machine learning models and their practical relevance
 ✅ Domain-focused insights on integrating AI into chemical industry workflows
 ✅ The importance of combining AI capabilities with expert domain knowledge

🔹 Session by Neeraj Sharma

Prof. Sharma introduced participants to key concepts underpinning modern AI systems, covering:
✅ Machine Learning conceptualization and fundamentals
 ✅ Deep Learning technologies and architectures
 ✅ Image processing and computer vision applications
 ✅ Speech processing technologies and AI-driven communication systems
 ✅ Overview of basic machine learning models and their real-world applications

The sessions provided participants with a deeper understanding of how AI and Machine Learning are being leveraged across industries, from manufacturing and chemical processes to computer vision and speech technologies.

As the program progresses, participants continue to strengthen their understanding of both the theoretical foundations and practical applications of AI, preparing them to drive innovation within their respective domains.
